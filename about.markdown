# Hi! I'm Dewi.
My path to becoming a Digital Trainer wasn't a straight line, but it’s been a rewarding experiment every step of the way. With a background in Biology, I spent my early career in the lab developing assays and training junior scientists—a role where I first discovered my love for breaking down complex systems into teachable moments. Eventually, my curiosity led me to Software Engineering, where I found that debugging a tricky block of code felt remarkably like the trial-and-error of a scientific experiment.

Today, I’ve traded the lab coat for a keyboard, but my passion for sharing knowledge remains at the heart of what I do. Having navigated a major career pivot myself, I find it incredibly fulfilling to empower other career-changers as they make their own leaps. I thrive on the flexibility of digital training and the 'lightbulb moments' that happen when a student masters a new challenge. To me, there’s nothing better than helping someone turn their professional aspirations into a reality.
